Anadolu Sofrası — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27.10.2026 · App: Anadolu Sofrası (iPhone and iPad, com.anadolusofrasi.oyun), published on the App Store in Türkiye.

1. Controller

Yusuf Doğan, doganyusuf967@gmail.com. This policy is written to comply with Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, for users in the EEA/UK, the GDPR.

2. Summary

  • No accounts, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device and never sent to us. A copy is backed up to your own iCloud account; we cannot access it and we run no servers.
  • The game shows ads: optional rewarded ads (you start them) and short interstitial ads at natural breaks, served by Google AdMob (§3.2). Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • If you own "Premium Paket" (Premium Pack), or the earlier "Reklamsız" (No Ads) product, no ads are shown, the ad SDK is never started, the tracking prompt is never shown, and no data goes to AdMob.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • Optional local notifications are scheduled on your device only.
  • Clock check: to stop build timers being skipped by changing the device clock, the game reads only the current date from Apple's or Google's web server (§3.5). The request contains no data about you.

3. Data processed

On your device: the save file (progress, settings, names you type), scheduled local notifications, the IDs of products you own, and an ad-frequency counter (total play time). None of it is sent to us. Settings → Kaydı sil (delete save) deletes the device copy and the iCloud backup (not your purchases).

iCloud backup: a copy of the save (a few KB) is written to Apple's iCloud key-value storage in your own iCloud account. No sign-up; it works when iCloud is on. Apple stores and encrypts it; we cannot see or access it. Purchases are not in it (use "Restore purchases").

Google AdMob (when an ad is loaded or shown): advertising ID (IDFA, only with tracking permission) and IDFV; IP address (coarse location); ad and app interactions (launches, ads seen, taps, video views); diagnostics (crash logs, launch time, SDK errors). Purposes: serving and measuring ads, personalised ads if you consent, fraud prevention, analytics. No precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. Ad requests are tagged as not child-directed and limited to "Teen (T)" content. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Tracking permission: on first launch Google's short explanation screen is followed by Apple's "Ask App Not to Track / Allow" prompt. Without permission, ads are non-personalised. No part of the game depends on this choice. Owners of "Premium Paket" (or the earlier "Reklamsız") never see the prompt.

Local notifications: if you allow them, a short reminder when a build finishes or your builders have been idle for a long time. Scheduled on the device, no push server, no notifications 22:30–08:30, no marketing. Permission is asked in context, never at launch.

In-app purchase: "Premium Paket" is bought through Apple's App Store (the earlier "Reklamsız" is still recognised). Apple processes payment; we only see, on your device, which product you own. Purchases can be restored; refunded items are removed.

Clock check (§3.5): on launch and when returning to the app, a body-less HTTPS HEAD request to https://www.apple.com (or https://www.google.com); only the Date response header is read. No identifier, advertising ID, save or game data is sent; the result stays on the device. Like any connection, these servers technically see your IP address under their own policies; we never receive it.

Anonymous usage analytics (GameAnalytics, §3.8): a small set of in-game events (event name, tutorial step, province index, seconds, game version, session number, OS name and major version) is queued on the device and sent over HTTPS to GameAnalytics ApS (Denmark), grouped by a random install ID generated by the app (not derived from the device, not an advertising ID, gone when the app is deleted). No name, email, hero/shop/staff names, advertising ID (IDFA/AAID) or precise location is sent; the data is not used for tracking or ads. Legal basis: legitimate interest in improving the game (KVKK Art. 5/2-f). You can turn analytics off with the "Anonymous usage data" switch in Settings; while off, no events are recorded or sent.

When you email us (support or "report an inappropriate ad"): we use your email address, your message and the device info added to the email (OS name and version) only to answer you, and delete it within [12] months after the request is closed. Emailing us is optional.

Legal bases (KVKK): explicit consent (Art. 5/1, 9) for personalised ads and the related transfer abroad, given in Apple's tracking prompt and withdrawable in iOS Settings; otherwise non-personalised ads based on performance of the service (Art. 5/2-c) and legitimate interest in fraud prevention (Art. 5/2-f).
